# V1.7.0 Physical Device Acceptance Checklist

**Status: All checks await real-device validation.** Chromium automation, virtual microphones, and mocked APIs do not count as Safari, AirPods, or Apple Music acceptance testing. This candidate must not be automatically merged or deployed.

Record tester, date, iPad model, full iPadOS/Safari version, AirPods model/firmware, browser or home-screen mode, microphone/audio output routes, and the initial music playback state. Test indoors at a safe, low volume; do not rely on the prototype in road traffic.

## A. Output-only Tone Preview (Detection Stopped)

- [ ] Test Standard and Test modes separately: while Apple Music plays, tap Preview once; music must continue, alert tones must be audible, and the original playback state must remain after preview. Record any volume ducking.
- [ ] If music is paused before preview, the preview must not start it.
- [ ] Check soft/normal/clear gain levels; tone frequencies and two-tone rhythm remain unchanged. Numeric gain values do not establish physical sound pressure levels.
- [ ] In Safari developer diagnostics, record `navigator.audioSession` availability and its `type` and state before/during/after preview. Even when ambient is supported, test actual music mixing; reading back a property does not prove success.
- [ ] If ambient is unsupported or rejected, Apple devices show a compatibility warning, create no preview AudioContext, and leave music unchanged.
- [ ] Without microphone permission, Preview must not request it, show active microphone usage, or start the model.
- [ ] Tap quickly 20 times, then repeat 20 times after playback ends; no overlapping residual tones or resource growth, and status remains accurate.
- [ ] Background, close tab, or lock the screen during the first tone; no residual audio or automatic replay on return, and manual operation remains available.
- [ ] Starting detection during preview stops preview and hands audio policy to detection. Preview during detection says detection must stop first and does not create another output session.

## B. Live Capture and Automatic Alerts (Independent Acceptance)

- [ ] Start real microphone/YAMNet capture while music plays. Record interruption, sound-quality change, and Bluetooth route changes; **passing section A does not imply passing B**.
- [ ] Indoors, play traceable real recordings of horns/bells/sirens as positive examples and ordinary sounds as negative examples. Cross-check classification, all eleven raw scores, effective thresholds, and actual alerts. Do not substitute simulated UI data.
- [ ] Verify threshold-triggered automatic tones, gain setting, mute, and current-warning display. Record whether AirPods/speaker output is actually audible.
- [ ] Microphone selection shows only inputs supplied by the browser; report selection/disconnection/reconnection faithfully. Do not claim the AirPods microphone was selected unless evidence confirms it.
- [ ] After 20 start/stop cycles, denied/revoked permission, incoming calls/Siri, or another app taking audio focus, old tracks and Workers must release without automatically restarting capture.
- [ ] Backgrounding/locking stops capture immediately; returning requires manual restart. During a 30-minute run record heating, battery use, dropped frames, and update interruptions.

## C. Both Modes and Accessibility

- [ ] At 1194×834, 1024×768, 1366×1024, 390×844, 320×568, and iPad Split View, rotation and Safari chrome expansion/collapse produce no horizontal overflow and keep controls reachable.
- [ ] Each Test-mode column scrolls separately; general settings, eleven sliders, individual/all resets, CSV controls, and log filters are touch-accessible.
- [ ] Standard omits Debug/Top 5/eleven-class diagnostic tables. Test-mode Top 1/5, model status, sample rate, and inference stats come from the current genuine run; unavailable values show N/A.
- [ ] Overall setting is consistent across both pages. Preserve six existing category preferences; five added categories default to 0.10. Overall default remains 1.00, and Reset All also restores the overall setting.
- [ ] Japanese/English/Simplified Chinese, light/dark/system themes; long microphone names remain readable; VoiceOver and keyboard focus work; touch targets are at least 44 px.
- [ ] Ten-second waveform is vertically symmetrical; ordinary portions are blue and only samples corresponding to warnings turn red; no red chart background blocks or Y axis.
- [ ] CSV distinguishes inference/warning, raw_score, model_inference_ms, and per-class thresholds; it excludes PCM/recordings, downloads only on request, and never uploads automatically. A new session clears in-memory logs.

## Release Gate

For each of A/B/C record pass/fail/limited and reproducible steps. Do not mark Apple Music compatibility resolved or merge/deploy until core sections A and B have been tested. Preserve inability to capture and mix audio simultaneously, if present, as a known system limitation.
